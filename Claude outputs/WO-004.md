## Stage A — M4a close-out

### A1. Gust reach consistency
The DM's diagnosis from the code (verify it, don't trust it):
1. The cone is ±22.5°: `Level.pushCone` halves `coneDeg` 45. Keyboard aim is 8-way, so off-axis targets are a knife-edge. With no Gust VFX yet, a miss reads as "too far".
2. Range is 3D distance from the body centre to ONE point (`pushTarget()`: windmill hub, pile centre, fan hub). So reach measured from each prop's *visible edge* differs:
   - debris ≈4.7 m (the target is the pile centre, 0.8 m inside its edge)
   - windmill ≈5.2 m horizontally (the hub sits 1.75 m above the gust origin)
   - fan ≈5.4 m
3. The gust is invisible until M5, so the player can't learn its shape.

Build:
- **(a) Ruling 2 resolved.** Rename `wind.gust.coneDeg` → `coneHalfDeg`, value **45** (±45°), marked **PROVISIONAL**. The VP judges it at his next play; his menu is 30 / 37.5 / 45. The rename is authorized; update SPEC §9 to match.
- **(b) Test range and angle against each pushable's bounds**, not its centre point. The same player-to-edge distance must register the same on the windmill, the debris and the fan; test that it agrees within ±0.1 m. The `pushTarget()` interface change is authorized, and you choose the representation. Report a before/after reach table.
- **(c) F3 also draws the Gust wedge** (range × half-angle) for the gust's duration, and flashes each pushable's gizmo hit or miss. Debug-only.

Range (5.5) and force (18) stay unchanged.

### A2. Colour and shape language
- The Shard gets a shape that is **not** the Core's octahedron.
- Its hue must sit outside all four element hues: Wind grey-blue, Fire red/orange, Water blue, Earth brown/ochre.
- DM candidate: a small **violet tetrahedron**. Show the VP a harness frame; he can veto it.
- Audit the palette. `accent` (#C2453A, red) is used only by the Shard; retire it or recolour it.
- Re-bless the affected sandbox goldens only after viewing them.

### A3. Record in DECISIONS.md and the tuning comments
- These provisional values become **"VP-approved 2026-09-27 (sandbox)"**: `player.externalDrag` 2.5, `props.updraft.force` 20, `windmill.spinPerForce` 0.5, `gate.openTime` 1.2, `fan` 14 / 4 / 4 / 9.5, `elements.hitStopScale` 0. They are still re-judged in Level 1 context.
- **Ruling 1 accepted:** `glide.horizontalDrag` is a speed multiplier.
- **Ruling 3 accepted:** `maxSpeedSafety` clamps AND counts. The Developer's reasoning was right: a count-only cap cannot stop tunnelling.

## Stage B — Decision record (docs only; no code)

Record the VP's decisions of 2026-09-27 in SPEC.md, CLAUDE.md and DECISIONS.md.

- **B1. Platform.** Web only for now. iOS is considered only after the full game's public release. The likely route is wrapping this web build, not a rewrite. Touch controls remain an anti-goal.
- **B2. Release.** No public release until the full game: Wind, Fire, Water and Earth, a level per element, then swap-puzzle levels. Fusion stays post-release (SPEC §2.5 unchanged). In SPEC §10, M7 keeps its polish work, but its public deploy moves to a final Release step after the full game (§12).
- **B3. Build order unchanged.** The Level 1 + Wind vertical slice (M4b → M5 → M6) must be complete before any second element exists. CLAUDE.md's anti-goal becomes: "A second element before the Level 1 + Wind slice (M4b–M6) is complete in the private preview."
- **B4. Private preview from M4b.** Builds go to a *restricted* itch.io page. No build is ever made public.
- **B5. New hard rule 11.** Gameplay reads semantic actions only (move vector, jump, action). It never reads key codes, mouse position, hover or right-click. Debug keys are exempt.
- **B6. New SPEC section: platform constraints.** These are constraints, not features:
  - aim stays forgiving;
  - the critical path uses generous timing, and precision lives only in optional content such as shards;
  - fps is checked in iPhone Safari at each milestone from M4b;
  - the M6 shell ships a swappable save backend (iOS Safari can evict site storage), audio unlocked by the title-screen press, a HUD inside the screen's safe area, and prompt glyphs that follow the last-used input device.
- **B7. Colour language** (a corollary of SPEC §1 pillar 3). Element hues and the Core shape are reserved for Cores and element effects. Nothing else may use them: not collectibles, not markers, not UI.
- **B8. WindZone mass rule** (SPEC §6.4). A zone's force F must satisfy `m_wind·drag·v_wind < F < m_base·drag·v_base`. The Wind body must lose a tug-of-war that the plain body would win. At drag 2.5 that means **10.8 < F < 16.25**. The current 9.5 fails the rule: a braced Wind body out-walks the wind (7.2 vs 6.3 m/s terminal), and Beat 4's twist disappears. A test enforces the rule when Beat 4's zone is authored. The disposable sandbox is exempt.
- **B9. M4b playtest units** (DM ruling). Beats 0–2 (the yard) are one unit. Beat 3, Beat 4, and Beats 5+6 are one unit each. The VP plays each unit before the next is built.
- **B10. M5 list gains an item:** checkpoint activation must read without words (the VP didn't recognise the pads).
