## Stage B — the crown rotor

SPEC §3, Crown rotor: *"Appears **only while gliding**. A thin spinning disc/blades at the `crown` socket."*

- **What.** A Wind `AttachmentSpec` on the `crown` socket, built from primitives. It is a thin disc with blades, so the spin reads from the isometric camera.
- **When.**
  - It is hidden unless the player's state is `glide`. `AttachmentSpec.animate` already receives `PlayerRuntimeState`.
  - It deploys with a short scale-in when the glide starts and retracts when the glide ends.
  - It is never visible after a respawn or a replay unless he is gliding.
- **Element-blind.**
  - Player.ts stays unchanged; it is at its 300-line limit.
  - No `if (element === …)` anywhere.
  - Everything lives in the Wind module plus tuning.
- **Values.**
  - Size, spin rate, deploy time and colour (if needed) go in `src/config/tuning.ts` under `wind`, all marked PROVISIONAL. The CEO judges the look.
  - The spin is time-based (rate × dt).
- **Readable.** In a gliding frame from the gameplay camera, it is clearly visible against both the sky and the grey level.
- **Leave alone.**
  - Leave the M4a test-fixture ring and everything else untouched.
  - Add no new debug toggle unless you see a need, and say why. The prompts set the precedent of none.
- **Tests.**
  - The rotor shows only while gliding.
  - It hides on landing, on respawn and on replay.
  - The spin is frame-rate independent.
- **Goldens.**
  - Frames where he glides will change.
  - View every changed frame, and bless only frames whose only change is the rotor.
- **Evidence** in `Claude outputs/WO-007-evidence/`:
  - a glide in the Vent Court;
  - a glide off the Crosswind Bridge.
