## Stage A — Level Complete card (pulled forward from M6)

Put it in `ui/LevelComplete.ts` (the SPEC §8.5 name).

- **Trigger:** the existing `levelComplete` bus event from the Goal.
- **The moment:** freeze the sim through the ONE TimeScale mechanism (MIN rule), then fade the screen toward the level's sky colour.
  - New tuning keys, all PROVISIONAL for the VP: `ui.levelComplete.fadeTime` (start at 0.5 s) and `ui.levelComplete.holdBeforeInput` (start at 0.6 s, so a held key can't skip the card by accident).
- **The card has NO WORDS** (SPEC pillar 3). Only icons and numbers:
  - a completion glyph;
  - the time, as a clock glyph + `m:ss.s`;
  - shards as a Shard glyph + `found/total`. Hide this row when the level has no shards.
  - a replay prompt: the Jump keycap glyph from Input.ts's bindings, following the last-used device, like the D3 prompt.
- **Timer:**
  - It counts **raw fixed-step time** from level load to the Goal. Pickup dilation and hit-stop must not shorten it.
  - Never read wall-clock time (hard rule 3).
  - It starts at load, not first input, because the card is how we measure SPEC §6.3's 4–7 minute first-timer target.
- **Replay:** pressing Jump (or Action) after the hold does a full level reset:
  - player back to spawn with a plain body (no element);
  - props, signals, shards and the timer all reset.
  - This must be the same state as a fresh load. Test it.
- The card is state-driven with no CSS animation (harness determinism, like the HUD). Add a golden of it.

## Stage B — Beat 3, the Vent Court (SPEC §6.2)

**The beat:**
- A floor vent choked with debris.
- Gust the debris, and the vent erupts into an updraft column.
- Step in, rise, then glide across to a high ledge.
- Use the existing props: Debris on the Updraft's grate, and the updraft `listensTo` the debris signal.
- Use the approved tuning: updraft force 20, rise cap 9 m/s.
- Target length ≈ 75 s for a first-timer (SPEC 1:30–2:45).

**Layout constraints (prove each one with a test or with frames):**
1. **Entry:** through the yard's gate. A **checkpoint** sits at the Beat 3 entrance (SPEC §6.3). Its activation feedback is M5; don't build it.
2. **Visibility — the hard one.** The yard's 7 m far wall hides anything behind it for roughly 8 m at this camera (pitch 38°, distance 16). The Vent Court must stay visible **by layout**: for example, the path climbs above the wall's sightline quickly, or the wall behind the gate is cut or lowered. No camera features (SPEC §4.4). Extend your raycast visibility test to the whole Beats 0–3 critical path, including the column ride and the glide.
3. **No void anywhere in Beat 3.** A missed glide drops him back into the court, so the retry is simply walking back into the column (§8.7: generous timing on the critical path).
4. **No sequence break:**
   - The ledge must be unreachable without the updraft + glide (highest reachable point without them < the ledge; tested).
   - It must be reachable with them by a **wide margin**. State the margin in the handback (glide distance needed vs available from the column top).
5. **Readability without words:**
   - The choked vent must look blocked.
   - The open column must be visible (the existing placeholder column is fine).
   - The ledge must be visible from the vent.
6. **The ledge is Beat 4's starting height.** Move the temporary Goal onto the ledge, so a full run is Beats 0–3, then the card.

**Harness:**
- Add `vent_court`: from the Beat 3 checkpoint → Gust the debris → ride the column → glide → ledge → card.
- Add or extend a full-run script: spawn → card, all four beats.
- View every frame, then bless. Existing yard goldens change only where the layout or Goal move touches them.
