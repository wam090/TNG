## Stage B — Beat 4, the Crosswind Bridge (SPEC §6.2: "the most important beat in the game")

**The beat:**
- A narrow bridge at the ledge's height, over nothing.
- A WindZone blows across it, perpendicular to the path, in pulses: the SPEC's 4.0 s period, 1.6 s gust, 0.6 s telegraph.
- The Wind body is light, so a gust blows him off.
- **Two discoverable solutions must both work:**
  1. Time the crossing between gusts.
  2. Use Gust in mid-air to counter the wind.
- A **checkpoint** sits at the bridge entrance (SPEC §6.3).
- Target ≈ 75 s for a first-timer.

**Constraints (prove each with a test or with frames):**
1. **The B8 mass rule, pinned by a test that uses simulated terminal speeds at the fixed 60 Hz step:**
   - the Wind body must **lose** the tug-of-war (bracing fully into the wind, he still drifts);
   - a plain body must **hold**.
   - At drag 2.5 the window is ≈10.6 < F < 15.9.
   - Start the zone force at the window's **midpoint (≈13)**, PROVISIONAL. The VP's menu is lower third / mid / upper third.
2. **Generous timing on the critical path (§8.7).** At Wind walk speed, the exposed span must be crossable within about **60 %** of the no-force window (calm + telegraph = 2.4 s). Do this with a shorter span, or with a sheltered pause point mid-bridge. Your call; state the margin.
3. **Both solutions proven by scripts:**
   - a timed crossing;
   - a mid-air Gust-counter that keeps him on the bridge through a full gust.
   - Recoil goes opposite the aim, so report which way he must aim (into the wind or with it). The VP judges whether that's discoverable.
4. **The liability is real.** A player who walks on during a gust with no counter-play gets blown off. Test it.
5. **Fast fail.**
   - Blown off → fade → respawn at the bridge checkpoint, target **≤ 1.0 s** from leaving the bridge to the fade (PROVISIONAL), whether or not he glides.
   - The global `respawnFallY` (−20) is far too deep here: a glide down would take ~8 s.
   - The mechanism is your call (e.g., a fall depth relative to the active checkpoint), with no new prop type and no magic numbers.
6. **No sequence break.** No route around the wind zone: no glide line around it or under it, no jump past it. Test it the way the yard and court are tested.
7. **Visibility.** Extend the raycast visibility test over the bridge, including the blown-off fall up to the fade.
8. **Readability without words.**
   - The telegraph must be visible before every gust (the placeholder haze is fine).
   - The bridge must read as narrow and dangerous.
9. **Move the Goal to the far end of the bridge** (Beat 5's start). A full run is Beats 0–4, then the card.

**Harness:**

- `crosswind_blown`: blown off → respawn at the checkpoint.
- `crosswind_counter`: the mid-air Gust-counter.
